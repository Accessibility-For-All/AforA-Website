Questions we always get

Is it really free?

Yes. A specialist reviews your site and you get the full Legal Risk Level report by email — no cost, no obligation, and nobody calls you unless you ask.

What happens after I submit?

We review your site against 50+ WCAG 2.1 AA criteria and email your report within one business day. It includes your risk level, the top violations we found, and what to fix first.

What are the actual legal deadlines?

Under the DOJ's ADA Title II rule (as extended in April 2026): state and local government entities with 50,000+ population must meet WCAG 2.1 AA by April 26, 2027; entities under 50,000 and special district governments by April 26, 2028. Private-sector websites face ADA Title III complaints with no fixed date.

Doesn't an accessibility widget make me compliant?

No. Overlay widgets don't make a site conform to WCAG, and sites using them appear in accessibility lawsuits. Compliance means finding and fixing the actual issues — which is what the audit shows you.

Who sees my information?

Only our team. We use your email to send the report and your URL to run the review — details in our privacy policy.
