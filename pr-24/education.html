Key Solutions for Education

Four solutions, one accessibility strategy — for districts, colleges, and universities.

Documents

Turn student handbooks, syllabi, financial aid forms, and board materials into accessible, searchable, translatable web pages with DocMersion.

Websites

District and campus sites, course catalogs, and admissions and financial-aid portals need to be accessible. Check gives you a free audit; Monitor keeps a constantly-changing site compliant automatically.

Reports

Ed-tech procurement increasingly requires a VPAT. Reports generates an automated Accessibility Conformance Report, with certified human review for a procurement-ready report your school board or accreditor can rely on.
