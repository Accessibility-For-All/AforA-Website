Built around Accessibility

We design the entire Accessibility For All suite around WCAG 2.1 AA, ADA Title II and Title III, Section 508, and international standards like EN 301 549 — because accessibility isn't a box you check once. It's an ongoing commitment to serving everyone.

No tool or vendor can promise that a site is permanently, fully compliant — standards evolve and content changes. What we can do is help you keep improving accessibility and stay on top of your obligations.

Our Commitment to Accessibility

At Accessibility For All, we believe digital accessibility is not just a legal requirement — it's essential to serving all people fairly, building trust, and fostering innovation. We treat it as continuous work, never a finished checkbox.

WCAG 2.1 AA

Our primary accessibility target — the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 AA, built to make content usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

ADA Title II & III

Americans with Disabilities Act guidelines, including the DOJ's 28 CFR Part 35 ruling, supporting equal access and opportunity for all users.

Section 508

Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act (36 CFR 1194.22) — the federal procurement standard we build toward for government and public-sector work.

EN 301 549

The EU's accessibility requirements standard, so organizations serving European users can meet international obligations too.

State-Level Standards

Including California's AB 434 and Unruh Civil Rights Act, and Colorado's HB 21-1110 — with more state requirements tracked as they take effect.

As standards and regulations evolve, we actively maintain and update the suite to match. Our platform is regularly audited by independent accessibility experts, and we aim to incorporate new requirements such as WCAG 2.2/3.0 as they are adopted.

Why Does This Matter?

Accessibility opens your services to everyone and reduces legal risk along the way.

Deep Dive into Compliance

Understanding the importance and implementation of digital accessibility standards.

What is digital compliance?

Digital compliance means working to make sure your website and digital documents meet legal and ethical standards for accessibility, privacy, and security. For public-facing organizations, this is not just best practice—it's the law.

Key Standards We Build Toward

WCAG 2.1 AA: our primary accessibility target — Web Content Accessibility Guidelines that help make content usable by people with disabilities.

ADA Title II and Title III: guidelines mandating digital accessibility for equal opportunity, including the DOJ's 28 CFR Part 35 ruling.

Section 508: of the Rehabilitation Act (36 CFR 1194.22), the federal procurement standard.

EN 301 549: the EU's accessibility requirements for organizations serving European users.

State-level guidance: including California AB 434, the Unruh Act, and Colorado HB 21-1110.

What happens without accessibility

People are shut out of services they rely on

Funding and grants tied to compliance can lapse

Trust and reputation erode over time

Global Standards & Beyond

Our commitment extends worldwide with comprehensive privacy protection and inclusive design practices.

Global accessibility laws and standards

DocMersion is designed to help organizations meet not just U.S. standards, but also international accessibility requirements such as EN 301 549 (EU), AODA (Canada), and more. We stay informed about global regulations to help you serve a worldwide audience.

Data privacy and confidentiality

Compliance isn't just about accessibility—it's about protecting your users' information. We follow best practices for data privacy and confidentiality, supporting regulations such as GDPR (Europe), FERPA (U.S. education), and more, so your documents and your users are always protected.

Inclusive design and user feedback

We believe compliance is a journey, not a checkbox. Our team works with real users—including those with disabilities—to test, refine, and improve our platform. Your feedback is always welcome and helps us build a more inclusive digital world.
