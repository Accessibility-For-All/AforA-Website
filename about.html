Meet the founder

A career built on one question

Stephen's graduate thesis at the University of Colorado was titled "What I Wish I Had as a Student." Two and a half decades later, it's still the question driving his work. Starting in 2000, Stephen has spent his career building technology that makes institutions easier to understand and easier to use.

He founded SchoolFusion, a first-of-its-kind K-12 communication platform, which was later acquired by Blackboard (now Anthology). In 2014, he founded SchoolBlocks to keep building on that mission — and Accessibility For All grew out of the same conviction: that the information an organization publishes only has value if everyone can actually access, read, and understand it.

That's the standard every product in this suite is held to.

Why we do this

An informed public makes for a better-functioning society

The more people can actually read, understand, and engage with the policies, records, and services that affect them, the better any institution — public or private — serves its community. We build the tools that make that possible, and we help every organization we work with understand how their own audience engages with their content, so they can keep improving.

Built for the public sector

Governments, schools, associations, and nonprofits — the whole suite is designed around what these organizations need.
