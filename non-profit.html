Key Solutions for Nonprofits

Four solutions, one accessibility strategy — for your community, your board, and the funders you rely on.

Documents

Turn grant reports, program materials, handbooks, and board minutes into accessible, searchable, translatable web pages with DocMersion.

Websites

Your donation pages and volunteer portal need to be accessible — for a mission-driven organization, that's not just legal risk, it's the mission itself. Check gives you a free audit; Monitor keeps a constantly-changing site compliant automatically.

Reports

Grant applications from foundations and government funders increasingly require or prefer a VPAT. Reports generates an automated Accessibility Conformance Report, with certified human review for a fully executed, procurement-ready report your funders can rely on.

Response

Most nonprofits have no dedicated compliance staff. Response rolls up document, website, and VPAT status into one monthly email of suggested next steps — an easy way to stay on top of things, included free to start.
