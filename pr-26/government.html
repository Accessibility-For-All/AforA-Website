Key Solutions for Government

One suite covering every corner of your agency's compliance obligations — from public records to procurement-ready reports.

Documents

The DocMersion toolkit turns ordinances, meeting minutes, and compliance forms into accessible, searchable, translatable public records.

Websites

Large, multi-department sites change constantly — new agendas, minutes, and forms. Get a free Check against the April 26, 2027 ADA Title II deadline, then let Monitor keep every page compliant as content changes.

Reports

Section 508 VPATs are a standard requirement in government IT procurement. Reports auto-generates your VPAT and Accessibility Conformance Report, with certified third-party review available for a fully executed, procurement-ready document.

Response

One monthly email rolls up every department's document, website, and VPAT status into suggested next steps — so a compliance or IT officer can show council members and auditors steady progress toward 2027, without dedicated in-house accessibility staff.
